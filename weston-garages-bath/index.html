Bay times below are typical, not promises — we confirm the real one when you book.

MOT testing

Class 4 and 7 tests in our own bay. If it fails we show you exactly what failed and what it takes to put right — no obligation to have the work done here.

Approx. 45 – 60 min

Full & interim servicing

Interim at six months, full at twelve. Manufacturer-schedule parts and oils, stamped service book, and your warranty stays intact.

Interim 1½ hrs · Full half a day

Engine diagnostics

Warning light on, running rough, or a noise you can't place. We read the fault codes, then actually test the circuit before condemning a part.

First hour diagnostic

Brake repairs

Pads, discs, calipers, lines and handbrakes. We measure what's left on the discs and tell you the honest answer — including when they'll last another year.

Most axles same day

Clutch replacements

Slipping, juddering or a pedal that's gone soft. Full clutch kits fitted, dual-mass flywheels checked while the gearbox is out rather than after.

Usually 1 – 2 days

Something else?

Cambelts, suspension, exhausts, batteries, air-con regas, welding for the MOT. If you can describe the noise, we can usually name it.
